# Sprint overview

Hasan started a 4-week AI engineering sprint on Sunday, October 11, 2026.
The goal is proof over promises: real builds, posted in public, instead of
tutorial certificates. He is an implementation analyst going deep on AI
engineering, so the things he builds hit harder in the job hunt.

Week 1 is about being findable: a LinkedIn overhaul, a build-in-public post,
and one shipped project. Week 2 is shipping proof. Week 3 is deploying it
live. Week 4 is hardening and consolidating everything into the resume.

The rule every week: one thing built, one thing posted, every week better
than the last. Proof plus visibility beats credentials.
